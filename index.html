The Root Cause is Simple

India doesn't have enough teacher training colleges — especially in rural and semi-urban areas. The few that exist are overcrowded, underfunded, or inaccessible to village youth. If we don't produce more trained teachers, these 11 Lakh+ seats will stay empty forever.

Our Solution

R & P Institute of Education:
A Teacher Training College

We're not just running tuitions. We're building a NCTE-recognized B.Ed & D.El.Ed college that produces certified teachers for India's villages.

The Vision

A Trained Teacher in Every Village

Our vision is that no village in India should be without a B.Ed or D.El.Ed certified teacher

The Reality Today

Children Are Eager. Teachers Are Missing.

In village after village, children sit in classrooms hungry to learn. But without trained teachers, their education is incomplete, inconsistent, and ineffective. Many schools operate with a single untrained instructor handling all classes.

  • 📉 40% of rural primary schools have fewer teachers than required
  • 🚫 Many village schools have ZERO B.Ed/D.El.Ed certified teachers
  • 📊 Student-teacher ratio in some areas exceeds 60:1
Our College Will

Produce 100+ Certified Teachers Per Batch

R & P Institute will run B.Ed and D.El.Ed programmes, producing government-certified teachers every year. These teachers will be specifically trained for rural school contexts — something mainstream colleges don't focus on.

The Future

Every Village Gets a Trained Teacher

Our graduates will be placed in village schools and eligible for government teacher recruitment exams (TET/CTET). We're not just educating — we're filling the 11 Lakh+ vacant posts that are starving India's children of their future.
